# Socio-Economics Pillar Engine Governance Rules

## 1. Social & Equity Provenance
- Gender labor coefficients, wage parity data, and social indicators must cite Central Bureau of Statistics (CBS) Nepal or official Ministry of Finance GESI frameworks.

## 2. Decoupled Autonomy
- Interactive models live under `engines/nexus/src/models/social/` and expose deterministic calculation interfaces.

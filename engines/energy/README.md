# Energy Pillar Computational Engines

## Mandate & Scope
The Energy Pillar models clean renewable power generation, energy access, and productive end-uses across Gulmi District:
1. **Hydropower Generation Sizing**:
   - Run-of-River (RoR) and micro-hydro capacity factor, turbine head loss, and monthly generation curves.
2. **Solar Photovoltaic Potential**:
   - NASA POWER solar global horizontal irradiance (GHI), tilt angle optimization, and rooftop/terrace PV yields.
3. **Bioenergy & Biogas**:
   - Anaerobic digestion from livestock dung and crop residue energy conversion.

## Modules Location
- Geospatial river reach physical screening is executed by `engines/water/hydro/`.
- Interactive renewable energy sizers and bioenergy calculators live in `engines/nexus/src/models/energy/`.

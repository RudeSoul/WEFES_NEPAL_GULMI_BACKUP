# Ecosystems Pillar Engine Governance Rules

## 1. Environmental Thresholds
- Environmental flows, soil degradation risks, and ecological buffers must align with Ministry of Forests and Environment (MoFE) and international environmental guidelines.

## 2. Decoupled Autonomy
- Standalone spatial ecology pipelines must reside in dedicated subdirectories under `engines/ecosystems/`.
- Interactive models live under `engines/nexus/src/models/ecosystems/` and remain pure functions without UI state.

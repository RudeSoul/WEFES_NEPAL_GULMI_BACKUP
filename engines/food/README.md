# Food Pillar Computational Engines

## Mandate & Scope
The Food Pillar encompasses agricultural, horticultural, livestock, aquaculture, and post-harvest value chain modeling for the WEFES Nexus platform:
1. **Agronomic & Crop Standards**:
   - NARC varietal characteristics, cropping calendars, sowing/harvest windows, and optimal thermal regimes.
2. **Crop Suitability**:
   - FAO EcoCrop multi-parameter envelope models evaluating temperature, precipitation, and soil reaction.
3. **Soil Nutrient Removal & Fertilizer Stoichiometry**:
   - Crop N-P-K extraction rates, organic farmyard manure nutrient availability, and chemical fertilizer requirements.
4. **Livestock & Dairy Dynamics**:
   - Cattle and buffalo feed intake, milk production curves, and enteric fermentation methane factors.
5. **Pest & Disease Surveillance**:
   - Agro-climatic risk indexing for mid-hill crop blights and pest vulnerabilities.
6. **Post-Harvest Loss Mitigation**:
   - Cold chain storage modeling and perishable commodity shelf-life extension.

## Modules Location
- Interactive models are located in `engines/nexus/src/models/food/` for real-time scenario simulation in the web frontend and API.
